Two Ways to Slim the Secondary Filters

Same position (between chips and cards), but lighter. Each tab uses a format that fits its content.

A

Inline Chip Row

A single row of compact toggle chips. Scrolls get scrollable chips, stimulants get stat badges, gems get gem-grade pills. No card frame, no heading — just the chips.

B

Compact Inline List

Retains checkbox labels but removes the card frame, background, and scroll container. Each tab is a lightweight inline row with small checkboxes and labels flowing horizontally.
