Slider Behavior: Filter vs Display-Only

Both show the same actual delta ranges from strategies — the difference is what happens when you drag the slider handles

A: Filter Mode

Sliders are interactive controls. Dragging the handles filters the strategy list — only strategies whose chaos deltas fall inside the selected range stay visible.

STR 1 4
DEX -2 1
🎯 Dragging STR to 1~4 and DEX to -2~1 hides strategies whose chaos scroll gave STR +0 or STR +5, or DEX +2 or DEX +3. Results update live.
Use case: "I only want strategies where the chaos scroll doesn't take too much from DEX"
B: Display-Only Mode

Sliders are read-only indicators. They show the full actual range of deltas across strategies but the handles are locked — you can't drag them. No filtering happens.

STR -3 +5
DEX -2 +3
📊 Shows the full spread — "Across all strategies, STR deltas range from -3 to +5, DEX from -2 to +3." All strategies still visible.
Use case: "Let me see what the chaos scroll did across all found strategies"
