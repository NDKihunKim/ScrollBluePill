Edge Cases Handled

No strategies loaded yet: No sliders shown — card just shows icon + name on the left. The right area is empty. Once strategies are computed, sliders populate.

Only 1 stat is target-relevant: Just one row appears (e.g., only STR). The card height shrinks accordingly.

All strategies show same delta for a stat: Slider shows min=max (both handles at same position), readout shows e.g. "2~2". Still draggable within chaos bounds.

Click to toggle off chaos scrolls entirely: Card deselects, sliders go to 35% opacity, all chaos-containing strategies are filtered out. Click again to re-enable.
